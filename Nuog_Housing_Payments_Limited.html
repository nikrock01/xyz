FAQ

Common questions

Who can apply?

Salaried employees (private or government) and doctors with valid ID, bank account and income proof. Doctor Loan is for registered medical professionals.

What documents do I need?

Aadhaar, PAN, bank statement and income proof (salary slips or ITR). Doctors also need registration and degree certificates.

How fast is disbursal?

After approval and KYC. Timelines vary; Instant Loan is the fastest.

Can I repay early?

Yes. Any applicable charges are shown in your offer.

How do I track my application?

Call or email us with your registered mobile number and we'll share the status.
