FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who can apply for a loan?

Salaried employees in private or government jobs, and doctors or other professionals with a valid ID, bank account and income proof.

What documents do I need?

Aadhaar, PAN, last 3 months' bank statement and income proof (salary slips or ITR). Home loans also need property documents.

How fast will I get the loan?

Disbursal happens after approval and KYC. Timelines vary by loan type and profile; Instant Loan is the fastest.

Can I repay early?

Yes, pre-closure is available. Any applicable charges are shown clearly in your offer.
